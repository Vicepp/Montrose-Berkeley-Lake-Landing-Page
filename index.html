The Opportunity

A well-located property with room to grow.

Montrose Berkeley Lake is a 492-unit garden-style community on 41 acres in Duluth, Georgia, at the center of one of metro Atlanta's fastest-growing counties.

The property is 94% occupied at an average rent of $1,387. Eighty units have already been renovated, with 412 left to capture the same upside.

This isn't a turnaround story. It's a working plan, partway done.
