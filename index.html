The Reveal

We haven’t shown you the numbers yet.

You have seen the property. 492 units on 41 acres in Duluth, Georgia, at the center of one of metro Atlanta’s fastest-growing counties. 94% occupied at an average rent of $1,387. Eighty units already renovated, 412 still to go.

That is the easy part to show. Photographs are honest about a building and silent about a deal.

What we have not put on this page is the arithmetic.

We do not publish the terms, because terms without context invite the wrong questions. We would rather walk you through them line by line, say out loud where the assumptions could be wrong, and take your questions while we are still on the call.

That is the whole reason the webinar exists. Come with your skepticism.
